## 句型 3｜by + 反身代名詞（自己一個人做）
- 頁碼：課本 p.15 Sentences、課本 p.18 A、習作 p.9 A、B
- 提示：by myself／by herself 是「自己一個人」；誰做這件事，就用誰的反身代名詞。

| 題型 | 題目 | 答案 | 其他選項 | 提示（可不填） |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 選擇 | Leo and Joe are playing cards (by) ___. | themselves | himself / them | |
| 選擇 | Ann went bowling (by) ___. | herself | himself / her | |
| 選擇 | He wants to fix his bike (by) ___. | himself | herself / him | |
| 選擇 | We can paint pictures (by) ___. | ourselves | themselves / us | |
| 選擇 | No. I don't want to take a walk (by) ___. | myself | me / yourself | |
| 選擇 | Can't you take a walk (by) ___? | yourself | you / himself | |
| 選擇 | Can your dog take a walk by ___? | itself | himself / it | 習作 p.9 A 第 4 題：dog 是動物，用 itself。 |
| 選擇 | My aunt went shopping by ___. | herself | itself / her | |
| 選擇 | The astronaut went to the moon by ___. (她) | herself | myself / themselves | 課本 p.18 A 第 1 題。 |
| 選擇 | My parents took a walk by ___ last night. | themselves | ourselves / yourselves | 課本 p.18 A 第 2 題。 |
| 選擇 | I enjoyed ___ at the party yesterday. | myself | me / I | 課本 p.18 A 第 4 題：enjoy myself 是「玩得很開心」。 |
| 挑正確 | | Her mother always cooks breakfast by herself. | Her mother always cooks breakfast by herselves. / Her mother always cooks breakfast by her. | 習作 p.9 B 第 3 題。 |

## 閱讀｜The Gentle Giant
- 頁碼：課本 p.17 Reading、習作 p.11 D
- 提示：按「看文章」，再讀一次文章找答案。

> **The Gentle Giant**
> Robert Wadlow was born in the USA. He was the tallest man in the world. He was eight feet and eleven inches tall (about 272 cm).
> He had two sisters and two brothers, but he was the tallest in the family. When he was thirteen years old, he became the tallest Boy Scout in the world. His father had to take away the front passenger seat in the car, so Robert could sit in the back seat.
> Robert was very nice and gentle. That's why everyone called him the "Gentle Giant."

| 題型 | 題目 | 答案 | 其他選項 | 提示（可不填） |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 選擇 | Who was the tallest man? | Robert Wadlow. | His father. / A Boy Scout. | |
| 選擇 | Where was he born? | In the USA. | In Spain. / In Taiwan. | |
| 選擇 | How many sisters and brothers did he have? | Two sisters and two brothers. | Two sisters and one brother. / He didn't have any. | |
| 選擇 | Was he a bad boy? | No. He was very nice and gentle. | Yes. He was very bad. / Yes. He was angry. | |
| 選擇 | How tall was Robert? | About 272 cm. | About 172 cm. / About 227 cm. | |
| 選擇 | Why did his father take away the front passenger seat? | So Robert could sit in the back seat. | So Robert could drive the car. / So Robert could sit in the front seat. | |
| 選擇 | Why did everyone call him the "Gentle Giant"? | He was very nice and gentle. | He was very angry. / He was a Boy Scout. | |
| 是非 | The tallest man, Robert Wadlow, was born in Spain. | 錯 | | |
| 是非 | Robert Wadlow doesn't have any sisters or brothers. | 錯 | | |
| 是非 | Robert Wadlow was a Boy Scout when he was 13 years old. | 對 | | |
| 是非 | Robert always sat in the front seat of a car. | 錯 | | 爸爸把前座拿掉，Robert 坐的是後座 back seat。 |
| 是非 | Robert was very nice to his girlfriend, so she called him the "Gentle Giant." | 錯 | | 是 everyone（大家）都叫他 Gentle Giant。 |

## 閱讀｜DIY
- 頁碼：習作 p.12 F Reading Pick
- 提示：按「看文章」，再讀一次文章找答案。

> **DIY**
> When most people in Taiwan hear the phrase "DIY," they think it means a teacher-led, one time experience in art classes. However, "DIY" also means the activity of fixing everyday problems by yourself. From stuck toilets to broken bicycles, there are a lot of small problems that you can fix yourself with the right tools and knowledge.
