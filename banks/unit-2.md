## 句型 1｜How much is it? / How much does it cost?（一個東西）
- 頁碼：課本 p.9 Grammar、習作 p.5 A
- 提示：一個東西用 is it／does it cost，回答 It is…／It costs…（cost 要加 s）。

| 題型 | 題目 | 答案 | 其他選項 | 提示（可不填） | 來源 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 選擇 | How much ___ the ring? | is | are / do | | 原題 |
| 選擇 | How much ___ the watch cost? | does | do / is | | 原題 |
| 選擇 | How much ___ the belt? | is | are / does | | 自編 |
| 選擇 | Q: How much is the ring? A: ___ one thousand, two hundred dollars. | It is | They are / I have | | 原題 |
| 選擇 | Q: How much does the watch cost? A: It ___ five hundred and ninety-nine dollars. | costs | cost / are | | 原題 |
| 選擇 | Q: How much is the hat? A: ___ eighty-nine dollars. | It's | They're / I have | | 自編 |
| 選擇 | The watch ___ 599 dollars. | costs | cost / are | | 原題 |
| 挑正確 | | How much does the watch cost? | How much do the watch cost? / How much does the watch costs? | does 後面的 cost 不加 s。 | 原題 |
| 挑正確 | | It costs five hundred dollars. | It cost five hundred dollars. / It are five hundred dollars. | | 自編 |
| 重組 | 它多少錢？ | How / much / is / it? | | 習作 p.5 A 第 1 題。 | 原題 |
| 重組 | 它要花多少錢？ | How / much / does / it / cost? | | 習作 p.5 A 第 2 題。 | 原題 |

## 閱讀｜Big Ben
- 頁碼：習作 p.8 D Reading Pick
- 提示：按「看文章」，再讀一次文章找答案。

> **Big Ben**
> Speaking of the best sights in London, Big Ben, the tower clock, must be one of them. Big Ben has a clock on each of the four sides of Elizabeth Tower. This giant clock is famous for its accuracy and it started ticking away on the 31st of May, 1859. From the top of Elizabeth Tower, it has told time in London for over 150 years.

| 題型 | 題目 | 答案 | 其他選項 | 提示（可不填） | 來源 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 選擇 | When did Big Ben start ticking? | On the 31st of May, 1859. | On the 31st of May, 1895. / On the 13th of May, 1859. | | 自編 |
| 選擇 | How many clocks does Big Ben have? | Four, one on each side. | One. / Two. | | 自編 |
| 選擇 | The clock shows 11:15. What time is it? | It's a quarter past eleven. | It's a quarter to eleven. / It's a quarter past twelve. | a quarter＝15 分鐘；past＝過了。 | 原題 |
